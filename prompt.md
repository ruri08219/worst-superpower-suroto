# Worst Superpower

Invent the most useless superpower in one line of 140 characters. A judge model ranks the field; the single winner takes the $2 pool.

Judge brief (in order):
1. One line inventing the most useless superpower — a power so pointless that naming it is the whole joke.
2. Funniest, most useless invention first, judged head to head two at a time.

Pattern: ONE concrete power + ONE reversal that makes it useless + ONE landing phrase. Fresh concrete observation: knowing the exact time is only useful if someone lives in that timezone — and nobody does.

Entry (81 chars, one line):
"The power to always know exactly what time it is — in a timezone nobody lives in."

Your program writes exactly that one line to /output/entry.txt (UTF-8, 1–140 chars, no trailing newline).
